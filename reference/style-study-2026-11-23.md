## What they all do (copy the FORMAT, never the content)
1. **"You" problem → fix chain.** "If you… / But… / So you… / But then… / So you…" — every 5-8 s a new problem, the fix adds
   one visible thing. Ending pays it off: "…you have built a <thing>" / the real-world name of it. (#1 #2 #3 #4)
2. **One set that grows.** A single place (yard, cut-away of the ground, a highway) where each fix is added on top, so the
   viewer watches it get built. Cross-section / cut-away views to show the inside. (#2 #3 #4)
3. **Progress HUD** (#1): small checklist "Build 3/6" with ticks + a counter of the key number. Strong retention.
4. **Keyword pops:** the key noun/number appears big and coloured on the exact word, with a short SFX hit (whoosh/pop/thud). (#1 #2 #4)
5. **Captions in the middle** of the screen (not low), 1-3 words, one keyword in colour.
6. **No music bed** in 3 of 3 measured — voice + many small SFX (≈ one every 2 s), loud mix (-14…-17 dBFS).
7. Pace: a new picture every ~2-3 s (or one slow continuous camera move per beat, #3/#4).
8. People appear as small working figures (soldiers, workers, crew) — not just a presenter.

## Where we are already better
Our Blender renders are more realistic (real materials, DOF, HDRI light) than #1/#2/#4, which look like a game. Keep it.

## Changes to make (owner decides which)
A. Script: always the problem → fix chain with a "you" hero and a "you have built…" ending (no code change).
B. Captions: move to the middle (y≈900-1000), 1-3 words, one keyword yellow.
C. Keyword pop labels (big, coloured, on the word) + an SFX hit on each.
D. Sound: drop or lower the music bed, add more small SFX (one per cut / pop).
E. Progress HUD (checklist + counter) for "build" topics.
