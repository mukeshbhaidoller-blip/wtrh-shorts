WHAT THE VIDEOS ARE
- Realistic 3D "how does it work" explainers: cars, machines, airplanes, engines, how houses and buildings are built.
- Must look REAL, like a movie or a car advert. Never cartoon, toy-like or video-game looking.
- A realistic human character appears in several shots, so the viewer feels he is inside the video.
- Vertical 9:16, about 40-60 seconds, white bold captions near the middle-bottom of the screen.

GOOD FRAME
- One clear main subject (the machine part or the person), sharp, well lit, easy to understand on a phone.
- Natural light and real materials: metal shines, rubber is matte, roads and walls have texture.
- The picture shows what the narrator is saying at that moment.

BAD FRAME (report these)
- Looks like a cartoon, plastic toy or old video game.
- Person's face, hands, arms or legs look broken, melted or pass through his body or through objects.
- Person floating above the ground or sunk into it.
- Caption covers the main subject or is cut off at the edge.
- Picture too dark to see, or washed out white.
- Nothing interesting in the frame (empty background only).
- Gore, blood, injury, politics, flags or real brand logos.
